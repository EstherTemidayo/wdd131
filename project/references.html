References and Inspirations

This page shows the project ideas and sources I used to build the TRUB website.

Why I Made TRUB

I wanted to make a travel website that helps people enjoy their journeys. The main goal is to make traveling comfortable, efficient, and easy to use without breaking the bank. With this system, you can book a safe car right from your house, travel across states, and feel safe using live tracking and our emergency helpline button.

Where I Got My Ideas

  • Uber: I looked at Uber to get ideas for choosing different types of cars, booking easily from home, and setting up clean layouts.
  • Bolt: I got ideas from Bolt for making travel affordable but comfortable, adding the hourly pricing setup, and building the safety warning section.
  • Base44 Travel Flow: I used this site (trub-travel-flow.base44.app) as a guide to see how a smooth, step-by-step progress tracking bar should look.

Fonts and Emojis

  • The text styling uses safe web fonts like Cambria, Calibri, and Georgia that are already built into laptops and phones.
  • All the icons on the site are standard emojis so the website loads fast on both phones and computers.

TRUB

TransitHub — Website Planning Document

1. Site Name

Name: TRUB (TransitHub)

Logo Concept: A modern transportation-focused logo that represents movement, connectivity, and reliable travel. The visual identity should communicate a connection between passengers, transportation agencies, vehicles, and destinations.

Reason for Selection: The name TRUB is short, memorable, and connected to the idea of a transportation hub. TransitHub represents a centralized platform where passengers can discover transportation options, compare agencies, book trips, make payments, and monitor their journeys.

2. Site Purpose

TRUB is a transportation marketplace designed to make transportation booking easier, safer, and more convenient. The platform connects passengers with transportation agencies and allows users to search for available routes, compare transportation options, book trips, make payments, and track active journeys.

TRUB focuses on improving the passenger transportation experience by bringing important travel information into one platform. Users can view transportation agencies, routes, vehicle types, pricing, safety verification, and GPS tracking availability before making a booking.

The current version of the website is a frontend demonstration using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

3. Target Audience Scenarios

  • Scenario A: I need to travel from one location to another and want to compare available transportation agencies, prices, routes, and vehicle options before booking.
  • Scenario B: I have already booked a trip and want to know where my vehicle is and monitor my journey using live tracking.
  • Scenario C: I want to book transportation online without having to contact several transportation agencies individually.
  • Scenario D: I want to know whether a transportation agency is verified and whether its vehicles support GPS tracking before I make a booking.

4. Color Schema

TRUB uses a modern transportation-focused color palette designed to communicate trust, movement, safety, and clarity.

Primary Color #0B1F33 (Deep Navy) Used for: Navigation, headings, major branding elements, buttons, and important interface sections.
Secondary Color #16A6A0 (Transit Teal) Used for: Call-to-action elements, active states, tracking indicators, links, and visual accents.
Accent Color #F4B942 (Journey Gold) Used for: Highlights, selected states, trip information, ratings, and attention elements.
Background Light #F7F9FC (Soft Cloud) Used for: Main page backgrounds, cards, sections, and clean content areas.

5. Typography

Plus Jakarta Sans — Headings

Applied to: Main website headings, page titles, navigation branding, section headings, buttons, statistics, and important interface elements.

Plus Jakarta Sans — Body

Applied to: Standard body text, transportation descriptions, forms, booking information, agency details, labels, and footer content.

6. Website Structure

Home

Introduces TRUB and allows users to immediately search for transportation.

Agencies

Displays available transportation agencies with routes, pricing, vehicle types, safety verification, and GPS tracking information.

Live Tracking

Allows passengers to view their active journey, vehicle location, trip progress, and safety information.

Book & Pay

Guides users through selecting a trip, entering passenger information, making payment, and receiving booking confirmation.
